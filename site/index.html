OUR STORY · EST. 11 JANUARY 2023

អំពីក្រុម RARA

United by service.
Moving forward.

RARA brings together Royal Cambodian Armed Forces alumni of the Australian Defence Force Academy and the Royal Military College – Duntroon.

ក្រុម RARA បង្កើតឡើងនៅថ្ងៃទី១១ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ២០២៣ ដើម្បីភ្ជាប់ទំនាក់ទំនងអតីតសិក្ខាកាម តាមរយៈការអភិវឌ្ឍជំនាញវិជ្ជាជីវៈ ការចូលរួមជាមួយសហគមន៍ និងមិត្តភាព។

Founded on 11 January 2023, the group connects alumni through professional development, community engagement and enduring friendship.

Its activities include support for schools, knowledge sharing, sport and the Dining-in tradition.
